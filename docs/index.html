A tiny friend that lives in your notch

Coucou watches your AI coding agents — Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Antigravity and more — lets you approve Claude Code's requests from the notch, chat with Anthropic, Google AI or OpenAI, and keeps an eye on Stripe, GitHub, Vercel, n8n and more. Free and open source.

Download for MacView on GitHub
Private by design. No account, no analytics, no tracking. Your keys stay in your Keychain, Windows Credential Manager or the Linux Secret Service. Read the privacy policy.

Requires macOS 15 or later. No notch? Mochi sits in a small bar at the top of the screen. The Windows download is paused for now: Microsoft Defender wrongly flags the unsigned installer, and it will come back once that’s fixed. Windows users can build it from source. Linux: a beta is out (AppImage, .deb, .rpm), download it here.
